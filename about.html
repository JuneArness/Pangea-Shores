About Pangea Shores

A consulting firm built on the belief that clear thinking and honest partnership help organizations move forward with confidence.

Who we are

Pangea Shores is a business consulting firm that helps leaders make sense of complex challenges and turn them into practical plans.

The name reflects how we see our work. Pangea was the single landmass that once connected every continent; shores are where land meets open water. We help organizations see how the pieces of their business connect, and prepare for what lies beyond the familiar.

Our philosophy

Strategy only matters if it changes what happens on Monday morning.

We favor plain language over jargon, focused priorities over long lists, and plans that reflect the people who will carry them out. We listen first, say what we see honestly, and keep the conversation centered on your goals rather than our methods.

Our approach

Each engagement follows a steady rhythm: listen, assess, align, act and adapt.

We start by understanding your organization and the people in it, then build a clear picture of where things stand. From there we help leadership agree on priorities, turn them into action, and refine the plan as results come in.

What we believe

  • Clarity beats complexity

    The best strategies can be explained simply and understood by everyone who has to deliver them.

  • People make the plan

    Lasting change depends on the people doing the work, so they belong in the conversation early.

  • Honesty builds trust

    Useful advice sometimes means saying the hard thing. We'd rather be candid than comfortable.

  • Progress over perfection

    A good plan acted on today is worth more than a perfect plan that never leaves the room.

  • Perspective matters

    Looking beyond your own walls, to markets, trends and other industries, often reveals the answer.

  • Growth should last

    Sustainable growth strengthens the organization rather than stretching it past what it can support.
